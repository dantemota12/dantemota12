### ⭐ Flagship project: RappiPlus end-to-end business diagnostic

End-to-end business diagnostic of RappiPlus: data cleaning in Python, profitability analysis, SQL funnel and cohort retention, A/B test on checkout UI, and a Power BI executive dashboard.
* **Highlights:** $\$51.97\text{M}$ revenue and $\$5.97\text{M}$ profit analyzed · funnel bottleneck located at checkout · 40–43% weekly retention across cohorts · checkout A/B test not significant ($p = 0.416$), so the rollout was not recommended.
* [View repository](https://github.com/dantemota12/rappiplus-business-diagnostic)
